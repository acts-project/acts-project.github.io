Detailed Description

A surface the navigator offers on top of the tracking geometry, whether the geometry holds it or not.

It is intersected on every step and targeted whenever it is closer than the next candidate of the geometry, which is held back, not skipped. A closer portal wins, and a tie goes to the geometry.
