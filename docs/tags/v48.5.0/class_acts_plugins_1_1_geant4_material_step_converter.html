Detailed Description

Converts Geant4 steps into recorded material tracks.

This is the Geant4 side of a material recording with geantinos: an Acts::RecordedMaterialTrack is started from a Geant4 track with startTrack, and every step of it through a material is added to it with addStep as one Acts::MaterialInteraction. The caller owns the tracks.
