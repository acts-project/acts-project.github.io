Detailed Description

Stepper that moves the track along a helix in closed form.

Each step reads the magnetic field at the start position and moves the track along the exact helix in that field. The transport jacobian is also closed form. It is the exact derivative of the helix step, so it treats the field as constant over the step and has no field gradient term.

The field at the end of a step is also the field at the start of the next step. The stepper uses it to estimate the position error from the field change along the step, and it adapts the step size to StepperPlainOptions::stepTolerance. An accepted step needs no extra field lookup.

The navigation estimates the distance to a surface with a straight line. A step of that length along the helix passes a surface perpendicular to the track by about |omega x T|^2 h^3 / 3, with omega = (q/p) B, and the next step goes back. A target aborter must accept an intersection that far behind the track. The default near limit of SurfaceReached is 100 um.

Note
The stepper propagates in vacuum only. It ignores volume material.
