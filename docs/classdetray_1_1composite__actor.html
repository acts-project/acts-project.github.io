Detailed Description

template<concepts::actor principal_actor_t = base_actor, concepts::actor... observers>
class detray::composite_actor< principal_actor_t, observers >

Composition of actors.

The composition represents an actor together with its observers. In addition to running its own implementation, it notifies its observing actors

Template Parameters
principal_actor_tthe actor the compositions implements itself.
observersa pack of observing actors that get called on the updated actor state of the compositions actor implementation.
