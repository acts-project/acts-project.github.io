Detailed Description

template<typename bounds_t, typename binning_t>
struct detray::axis::single_axis< bounds_t, binning_t >

A single axis.

An axis ties bounds and binning behaviour with the bin edges storage. The bounds determine how bin indices are mapped at the over- and underflow bins. The type of binning determines whether the axis has regular or irregular binning. The bin edges needed to find bin indices are not owned by the axis, but are passed to the binning type.
