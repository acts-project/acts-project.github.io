Detailed Description

Built-in RZ grid spatial index.

Construct after geometry closure, once bounds, hierarchy and descendant policies are finalized; prefer installing through a Blueprint or StaticBlueprintNode lookup factory. Rebuild after changing volume bounds, transforms, hierarchy or descendant lookup policies. Indexes the full subtree, stopping at explicitly configured descendant lookups. Child volumes must be contained within their parents. Unsupported bounds are checked linearly alongside indexed candidates.
