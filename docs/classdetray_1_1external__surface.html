Detailed Description

template<typename algebra_t, typename shape_t, typename material_t, typename nav_link_t>
class detray::external_surface< algebra_t, shape_t, material_t, nav_link_t >

Surface descriptor used for geometry supplied outside the detector store.

It bundles placement, mask, material, and detector identifiers.
